==================
a comment-only program retains its comment
==================

# comment only

------------------

(program
  (item_list
    (newline_opt
      (newline)
      (comment)
      (newline))))

==================
backslash inside a comment does not continue its raw newline
==================
# backslash stays in comment \
BEGIN {}
------------------

(program
  (comment)
  (item_list
    (newline_opt
      (newline)))
  (item
    (pattern
      (special_pattern
        (begin_keyword)))
    (action)))

==================
comment at EOF excludes the absent newline
==================
# comment at EOF
------------------

(program
  (comment))
